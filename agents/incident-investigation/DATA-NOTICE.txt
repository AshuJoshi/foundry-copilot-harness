Preparation selects a recorded case, converts Parquet tables to CSV, adds
evidence_row_id, and splits the complete trace table into two row-preserving
shards. No sampling, imputation or diagnosis is performed. Upstream case names,
fault labels and injection timestamps are excluded from model-facing packages.
These are controlled fault experiments, not production customer outages.
