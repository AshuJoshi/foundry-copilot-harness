ExampleCo, every statement below, the source titles and the source timestamps

In this fictional case, ExampleCo describes a service for extracting supplier
catalog information into structured records. Its product and pricing pages are
represented by [S1](https://example.com/product) and
[S2](https://example.com/pricing).

## Illustrative scenarios

These values come from the sample calculator, not from ExampleCo. The assumptions
are 10,000 monthly attempts, USD 0.06 per successful execution and USD 200 monthly
fixed cost, with different success rates and per-attempt costs.

| Scenario | Successful executions | Revenue (USD) | Variable cost (USD) | Gross margin | Contribution after fixed cost (USD) |
|----------|-----------------------|---------------|---------------------|--------------|-------------------------------------|
| Downside | 7,000 | 420 | 250 | 40.48% | -30 |
| Base | 8,500 | 510 | 180 | 64.71% | 130 |
| Upside | 9,500 | 570 | 120 | 78.95% | 250 |

Under these assumptions, the base case covers fixed costs while the downside
case does not. Actual company results would require actual pricing, workload
and cost data.

## Questions to investigate

1. What proportion of extracted records require human correction?
2. How does cost per accepted record compare with the current process?
3. Which supplier formats cause failures, and how are those failures handled?

## Unknowns and limitations

Revenue, customer retention and actual workload performance are unknown in this
fictional case. The four source records demonstrate citation structure only.
They are not Web IQ results. A real research brief needs actual source review
before its conclusions inform a decision.
