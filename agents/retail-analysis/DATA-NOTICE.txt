Derived pilot: original United Kingdom invoice lines in four fixed seven-day
windows in September/October 2010; source workbook/sheet/row identifiers added;
timestamps and values serialized to CSV; exclusion flags and period labels added.
Rows are preserved, including duplicates, cancellations and missing values.
No synthetic transactions, inventory, shipments, costs or cancellation reasons
are generated. Stage snapshots reveal chronological subsets only.
No endorsement by the data creator or UCI is implied.
This is historical non-store retail data, not current company reporting,
audited revenue, payment settlement, profit or a supply-chain event ledger.
